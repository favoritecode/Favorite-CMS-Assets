## Basic Usage Rules

1. **Light Backgrounds**:
   - Use the standard logo (`#0F172A` text, `#2563EB` "CMS" and spine, `#0EA5E9` wing).
   - Ensure a minimum contrast ratio of `4.5:1` for body text and `3:1` for UI components.
2. **Dark Backgrounds**:
   - Use the dark-adapted palette: `#FFFFFF` for "Favorite", `#3B82F6` for spine / "CMS", `#38BDF8` for wing, `#94A3B8` for tagline.
   - Avoid dark grey logos on dark backgrounds.
3. **Sub-Brand Expansion**:
   - The primary spine remains Favorite Blue or neutral dark; only the extension wing adopts the ecosystem accent color.
