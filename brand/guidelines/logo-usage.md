# Favorite CMS — Logo Usage Guidelines

Rules and best practices for presenting the Favorite CMS logo assets consistently and professionally.

---

## Logo Formats & Configurations

1. **Horizontal Logo (`favorite-cms-logo-horizontal.svg / .png`)**:
   - Primary lockup for website headers, repository banners, documentation navigation bars, and marketing footers.
2. **Stacked / Primary Logo (`favorite-cms-logo.svg / .png`)**:
   - Balanced vertical lockup for splash screens, marketing collateral, hero graphics, presentations, and social media profile cards.
3. **Standalone Logo Mark (`favorite-cms-logo-mark.svg / .png`)**:
   - For application icons, system notifications, avatars, dashboard dock icons, and micro-branding where space is limited.
4. **Standalone Wordmark (`favorite-cms-wordmark.svg`)**:
   - Exclusively for editorial layouts, copyright notices, or co-branding arrangements where the mark is displayed elsewhere.

---

## Clear Space & Sizing

### Minimum Clear Space
- Always maintain an isolation zone around the logo free from other graphic elements, borders, or typography.
- Clear space is proportional to the height of the letter **"F"** in the wordmark (designated as unit **`X`**).
- Minimum clear space is **`0.5X`** on all four sides of any logo configuration.

### Minimum Recommended Sizes

| Configuration | Minimum Digital Size | Minimum Print Size |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Horizontal Logo** | `140px` wide | `35mm` wide |
| **Stacked Logo** | `90px` wide | `24mm` wide |
| **Standalone Mark** | `24px × 24px` (`16px` for favicons) | `6mm × 6mm` |

## Background & Contrast Usage

- **Light Backgrounds**:
  - Always place the standard full-color logo on white, light grey (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`), or very subtle neutral tints.
  - The word "Favorite" is rendered in Ink Navy (`#0F172A`) and "CMS" in Favorite Blue (`#2563EB`).
- **Dark Backgrounds**:
  - On dark surfaces (`#0B0F19`, `#0F172A`, `#1E293B`), render the word "Favorite" in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and "CMS" in Electric Blue (`#3B82F6`).
  - The mark uses Electric Blue (`#3B82F6`) for the core spine and Bright Cyan (`#38BDF8`) for the modular wing.
- **Monochrome & High-Contrast**:
  - For single-color media (receipts, thermal printing, laser engraving, white-on-black stencils), the entire mark can be rendered in 100% flat black or 100% flat white. The negative-space gap between the spine and the crossbar guarantees legibility without color.

---

## Prohibited Modifications & Restrictions

To maintain brand integrity, never alter the logo in the following ways:

- **Do Not Distort**: Never stretch, condense, skew, or alter the proportional aspect ratio of the mark or wordmark.
- **Do Not Rotate**: Never rotate the mark or lockups at an angle.
- **Do Not Rearrange**: Do not reposition the mark relative to the wordmark or change spacing distances.
- **Do Not Re-color Randomly**: Do not swap colors, apply rainbow gradients, or introduce unapproved hue combinations.
- **Do Not Add Effects**: Never apply drop shadows, 3D extrusions, outer glows, bevels, or heavy filters.
- **Do Not Enclose in Cluttered Badges**: Do not place the mark inside arbitrary geometric enclosures (circles, stars, crests) unless strictly required by app store icon standards.
- **Do Not Place on Busy Backgrounds**: Avoid placing the logo directly over complex, low-contrast photos or busy patterns without a solid or heavily blurred backdrop.
- **Do Not Replace Typography**: Never re-type the wordmark using unapproved or generic fonts. Always use the provided vector assets.
